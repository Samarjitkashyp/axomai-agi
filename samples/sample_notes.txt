# Assam Tourism & Wildlife Overview

Assam is located in northeastern India. It is famous for Kaziranga National Park, home to the one-horned rhinoceros.

## Major Attractions
1. Kaziranga National Park - Wildlife and Rhinos
2. Majuli - The largest river island
3. Kamakhya Temple - Ancient pilgrimage site in Guwahati
4. Manas National Park - Biosphere reserve and tiger project

## Tea Gardens
Assam produces the world famous Assam Black Tea, grown along the Brahmaputra valley.
